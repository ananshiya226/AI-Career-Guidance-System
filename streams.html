Engineering

One of the most popular streams in India with a wide range of specializations.

Engineering is a broad field that involves applying science and mathematics to design and build things — from software to bridges to machines. After 12th Science (PCM), students can appear for JEE or state entrance exams to join a B.Tech / B.E. program. It is a 4-year degree offered in hundreds of colleges across India.

Popular Branches

  • Computer Science (CSE)
  • Electronics & Communication (ECE)
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • AI & Data Science

Career Options

  • Software Engineer
  • Data Scientist
  • Mechanical Designer
  • Civil / Structural Engineer
  • Embedded Systems Engineer
  • Project Manager
  • Startup Founder

Entrance Exams

  • JEE Main & Advanced (IITs/NITs)
  • State CETs (MHT-CET, KCET, etc.)
  • BITSAT (BITS Pilani)
  • VITEEE, SRMJEEE

Subjects in 11th-12th

  • Physics, Chemistry, Maths (PCM)
  • Some colleges accept PCM + CS
  • Strong Maths foundation is essential

This stream is suitable for you if...

You enjoy Mathematics and Physics, like solving logical problems, are interested in how things work, and want to build software, machines, or systems. You should be comfortable with 4 years of technical study and entrance exam preparation.

Pure Science

For students who love understanding how the natural world works.

Pure Science is for students who are fascinated by the fundamental principles of nature — physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics. It leads to research, academia, medicine, and a wide range of professional careers. A B.Sc. degree is typically 3 years, or students can pursue M.Sc. and Ph.D. for advanced research roles.

Popular Courses

  • B.Sc. Physics / Chemistry / Biology
  • B.Sc. Mathematics / Statistics
  • MBBS (Medicine)
  • B.Pharmacy
  • Nursing (B.Sc. Nursing)
  • B.Sc. Biotechnology

Career Options

  • Doctor / Surgeon
  • Research Scientist
  • Pharmacist
  • Biotechnologist
  • University Professor
  • Environmental Scientist

Entrance Exams

  • NEET (for MBBS/BDS)
  • CUET (for central university B.Sc.)
  • JIPMER, AIIMS entrances
  • State B.Sc. admissions

Subjects in 11th-12th

  • PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • PCM for maths-based science
  • PCMB for both options

This stream is suitable for you if...

You are curious about how the human body works, how chemicals react, or how the universe is structured. You enjoy lab experiments, reading science articles, and want to contribute to research or healthcare. You do not mind spending many years in study and specialization.

Commerce

For students interested in business, finance, and how economies work.

Commerce is a great stream for students who are interested in business, accounting, economics, and the financial world. It opens doors to careers in banking, finance, management, and entrepreneurship. The main courses after Commerce include B.Com, BBA, CA, and MBA after graduation.

Popular Courses

  • B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)
  • BBA (Business Administration)
  • CA (Chartered Accountancy)
  • B.Com + LLB (Law)
  • BMS (Business Management)
  • Economics Hons.

Career Options

  • Chartered Accountant (CA)
  • Business Analyst
  • Investment Banker
  • Entrepreneur / Startup Founder
  • HR Manager
  • Tax Consultant

Entrance Exams

  • CUET for B.Com / BBA
  • IPMAT (IIM Indore / Rohtak)
  • SET, DU entrance exams
  • CA Foundation (ICAI)

Subjects in 11th-12th

  • Accountancy
  • Business Studies
  • Economics
  • Mathematics (optional but helpful)

This stream is suitable for you if...

You enjoy working with numbers in a business context, like the idea of running a company or managing accounts, and are interested in how markets and economies function. You are good at planning, organising, and managing resources. Maths at the basic level is useful but not mandatory for all Commerce courses.

Arts & Humanities

A diverse and creative stream with many underrated career paths.

Arts and Humanities is often underestimated but it offers some of the most interesting and meaningful career paths. It covers literature, history, psychology, political science, sociology, design, media, and more. Students who choose this stream are often excellent communicators, creative thinkers, and empathetic leaders.

Popular Courses

  • B.A. Psychology / Sociology
  • B.A. English / Journalism
  • B.Des (Design)
  • B.A. Political Science
  • B.F.A. (Fine Arts)
  • B.A. LLB (Law)
  • Mass Communication

Career Options

  • Journalist / Content Writer
  • Graphic / UX Designer
  • Psychologist / Counselor
  • Lawyer / Advocate
  • Civil Services (IAS/IPS)
  • Social Worker / NGO Leader

Entrance Exams

  • CUET for central universities
  • NID / NIFT (for Design)
  • CLAT (for Law)
  • UPSC (for Civil Services)

Subjects in 11th-12th

  • History, Political Science
  • Psychology, Sociology
  • Geography, Economics
  • English Literature
  • Fine Arts / Music (optional)

This stream is suitable for you if...

You are a creative thinker who loves writing, designing, understanding people, or working in media and communication. You are not particularly drawn to Maths or Science but you have strong language skills, social awareness, and are passionate about making an impact through ideas and storytelling.
